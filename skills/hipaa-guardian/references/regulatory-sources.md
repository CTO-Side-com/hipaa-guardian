## Interpretation constraints

- HIPAA applies to regulated entities and appropriate business associates/subcontractors. Validate product-specific legal role and contractual scope.
- HIPAA Security Rule requires a risk analysis across **all ePHI** in the organization's environment. A repository review does not by itself satisfy it.
- `Required` and `Addressable` are classifications of implementation specifications. All standards must be met. For addressable specs, follow the evaluation/documentation/alternative process under 164.306(d)(3); not simply skip.
- The six-year duration in 164.316(b)(2)(i) concerns **required Security Rule documentation**, not a universal six-year retention mandate for patient records.
- BAA presence does not automatically authorize every feature of a provider. Require product-specific, account-specific scope and supporting contractual evidence.
- Verify proposed versus finalized Security Rule amendments. As of this review, HHS's history page presents the January 2025 cybersecurity strengthening rule as **proposed**; do not assert that proposed safeguards are legally effective without verifying subsequent Federal Register final-rule publication and effective dates.
- When reviewing privacy rights, marketing consent, recording laws, text messaging rules, state privacy law or cross-border transfer, clearly label the additional legal work; HIPAA technical compliance alone does not settle those matters.
